- Margin Discipline (factor 10) measures how far the rows' starts stray
  from the margin as the mean distance from the median start, in shares
  of the page width, and leaves out a row that starts far right of the
  others: a centred title, a date or a signature (#201). The coefficient
  of variation it replaces divided by the mean start, so the same spread
  scored worse the nearer the writing sat to the page's edge, and one
  date at the right took a page to 0.

- The free tier allows 3 checks a day per IP address (owner decision, 2026-10-09; `backend/daily_limit.py`). `/ocr`, `/analyze-vl`, `/report-python` and `/api/v2/reports` share one allowance; only a check that produced a result counts, so a refusal costs nothing. The day turns over at midnight India time. The fourth check gets HTTP 429 with `error_code: daily_limit` and `Retry-After`; the analyser page says "Today's free checks are used", why, when the count starts again, and links to the practice worksheets and the team, and reports the outcome as `daily_limit`. A Pro access key is never limited. CI and the docker network are not counted, nor the addresses in `VAHINI_DAILY_LIMIT_EXEMPT` (the server's own stage checks). `error.schema.json` and API-V2.md describe the 429.

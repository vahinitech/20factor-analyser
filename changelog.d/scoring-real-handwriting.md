- Every handwritten line is now scored once. Tested on 20 real writers' pages from UJI Pen Characters (CC BY 4.0), the reader scored 50 of 80 written lines; it now scores 77, none read twice. Four changes: two readings of one line no longer both survive when they differ by a letter ("seeta" and "seetha"); a wide handwritten line in the top 14% of the photo is no longer dropped as a header unless it looks printed; writing the detector missed is cropped and read again by the scan's own engine (`line_recovery.py`); and the pieces of one line are joined (`detector.join_row_pieces`). Recovered words do not change the classifier's evidence that a page is a printed form.
- Word Spacing no longer scores 0 when a line was read in pieces. The space between two boxes on one row was counted as a word gap, and when the words between them were never read it was seven line heights wide; gaps now come only from the ink inside each line, and only from blobs in the body of the line box, not the tails of the lines above and below. Fewer than three gaps is still "Not measured".
- Size Consistency measures each word's small-letter height as the median, over its columns, of how high the ink reaches above the baseline. The densest band of rows ran up into the loops of tall letters, so on joined writing "the", "day" and "dog" measured up to 1.6 times their line and were marked "taller".

- The check page asks who is checking: "I am" a parent or guardian (the
  default), a student, a teacher or checking my own writing. A student gets
  the student view, everyone else the parent report. "Report in" picks the
  report's language before the check. Both stay in the browser and are
  never sent. Beside the report only "Print in" is left, and a "Student
  view" or "Parent report" link under the report shows the other view of
  the same check.
- While a photo is checked, the page sweeps it line by line: the lines are
  the photo's own, found in the browser, with "Reading line 3 of 4" and one
  bar a line, until the report opens. People who turn off motion see the
  line marked without the sweep.
- "What's new" shows the latest ten changes in turn on one line, one every
  five seconds, with "1 of 10" beside it.

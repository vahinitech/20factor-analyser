- The letter facts the scorer used to write out in each module now live in language packs, `backend/languages/` (docs/20-languages.md, step 1 of the build order, #206). `scripts/Latn.json` holds the Latin script (letters, capitals, loop, bowl, tall and small letters, the t-bar pairs, `direction: ltr`) and `langs/en.json` the English language (word length, finishing letters, form words, its benchmark set). Each pack says for every field whether it sets, adds or removes; the registry checks the result against `pack.schema.json`, hashes it and caches it. `scoring.py`, `zone_analysis.py`, `letter_geometry.py`, `tbar_analysis.py`, `finishing_letters.py`, `style_analysis.py`, `coach_tips.py`, `classify.py`, `computer_vision.py` and `line_recovery.py` read the pack instead of their own lists. The packs hold exactly the old values, so no score moves. A new test fails when one of those modules writes a letter list, `[A-Za-z]`, a Unicode script range or `isascii()` again, CI resolves every pack, and the benchmark replays each pack's own set.

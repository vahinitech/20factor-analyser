- The server runs at most `VAHINI_MAX_ACTIVE_SCANS` scans at once (default 2), queues `VAHINI_MAX_QUEUED_SCANS` more (default 4) and answers 503 with `Retry-After` beyond that, instead of running the host out of memory: four simultaneous scans had the stage analyser killed by the kernel. Cache hits are never queued. The browser tells the visitor the analyser is busy rather than unreachable. How many scans run at once is now worked out from the host (`backend/capacity.py`): the container's memory limit or the host's available memory, usable cores and GPUs in use, from measured per-scan costs, and each slot gets its own OCR engine copy on its share of the cores. `/health` reports the plan as `scan_capacity`.

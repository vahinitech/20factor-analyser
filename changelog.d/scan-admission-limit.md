- The server runs at most `VAHINI_MAX_ACTIVE_SCANS` scans at once (default 2), queues `VAHINI_MAX_QUEUED_SCANS` more (default 4) and answers 503 with `Retry-After` beyond that, instead of running the host out of memory: four simultaneous scans had the stage analyser killed by the kernel. Cache hits are never queued. The browser tells the visitor the analyser is busy rather than unreachable.

- Pillow 13.0.0 (was 12.3.0) for the 82 open Dependabot alerts against it, 10 of them critical; Pillow decodes every uploaded photo. Pillow 13 resizes an image vertical-first when its height shrinks more than twice as much as its width (python-pillow/Pillow#9549), which rounded the pixels of a downscaled photo more than twice as tall as wide differently and moved its scores. `vision.geometry.resize` runs the two passes in Pillow 12's order, so a page photo of any shape gives the same working pixels and scores as before; the working copy (`to_numpy`) and the Telugu reader's crops use it, and `test_image_formats.py` pins the 12.3 pixels for both.

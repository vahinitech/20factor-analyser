- Ascender/Descender Control (factor 6) is measured against a page's
  printed guide lines when it has them (a worksheet's ruling or a
  four-line notebook, #194): each line's small-letter band comes from
  the ruling, and its reach is the median over its tall letters, so it
  no longer moves with whatever else changes in the line. On planted
  ruled pages the long ascenders are caught on 0.85 of writers (0.60
  measured from the ink) and two pages of the same passage differ by
  1.31 at the 90th percentile (5.33). Pages without ruling are measured
  as before.

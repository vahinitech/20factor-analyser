- A real-handwriting benchmark checks every pull request: 20 UJI Pen Characters pages (CC BY 4.0), the reader's output kept for each, replayed through the change by `scripts/benchmark.py check` in a new CI job with no models. Any score that moves fails until `benchmark.py update` is committed, so the PR diff shows every page and factor that moved; lines scored, a line scored twice and a measured 0 are limits that cannot be updated away. `benchmark-live.yml` reads the pages again with the real models when the reader can change, weekly and by hand. Planted-mistake rates are checked the same way. docs/18-benchmark.md.
- Margin Discipline no longer scores 0 when a piece of a line was read on its own: only lines that start a row count as left edges, not a piece with another line or unread writing to its left. The benchmark found this on its first run (UPV_W21 and UPV_W30).
- Word Spacing's evidence crop and marks no longer point at the space between two line boxes, which is not scored; they show the line holding the ink gap that stands out.

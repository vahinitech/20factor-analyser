- A neat reference set of engraved model handwriting from two
  public-domain penmanship manuals (Palmer 1915, Zaner 1925), with the
  live reader's output, for the calibration tool (#197). It is a
  reference for setting scales, not a scale to apply, and no score
  changed. It showed that the reader reads joined, engraved cursive in
  fragments (26 of 39 lines), so on most factors the measurement, not
  the scale, holds such pages down.
- `scripts/score_ranges.py` scores every factor on neat, UJI, clean
  and poor pages side by side; the planted-page maker can now put
  several mistakes on one page (`planted_pages.POOR`).

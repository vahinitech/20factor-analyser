- Passage alignment now compares a line with its passage letter by letter as a reader sees them, with vowel signs and viramas kept (#209). The old comparison dropped every combining mark, so a Telugu or Hindi line with a vowel sign missing matched its passage perfectly: పిల్లి compared as పలల and हिंदी as हद. A new grapheme splitter (`backend/languages/graphemes.py`, standard library only) keeps each Telugu or Devanagari akshara, conjuncts included, as one unit. English matches are unchanged; the one change for Latin text is that an accent typed as a separate mark now matches the same accented letter. New tests hold the grapheme counts of a Telugu and a Hindi line, the lower match for a missing vowel sign, and that a passage cannot raise Loop Closure or Letter Zones on the same ink; the two cases where today's scoring still counts the passage's letters are marked as expected failures until scoring counts those shares from the reading.

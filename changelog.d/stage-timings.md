- Every scan now records where its time goes (`backend/perf.py`, #229): wall time, CPU time, the change in the container's memory and the call counts of each step, from decoding the photo to building the practice report. A scan answers with a `Server-Timing` header (step names and milliseconds), `/health` shows each step's p50 and p95 over the last 512 scans as `stage_timings`, and the server log gets one line of numbers per scan. No text, picture, name or address is recorded, and no number changes what is read or scored.
- `backend/perf/budgets.json` says how much one page may cost: reader calls at most lines times rungs, one batched model call per rung, a decoded photo of at most 24 MP, at most 24 line recovery reads, and a CPU cap per step. CI replays the benchmark pages through the server's own scan and fails when a page breaks one (`scripts/perf_budgets.py`); in production a breach is only counted in `/health`.

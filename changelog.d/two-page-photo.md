- A photo of two facing pages (an open notebook, from the camera or the
  gallery) is now read as one page: the left page, or the right one when
  the left has no writing. The binding between the pages is found by
  counting (a band darker than the paper on both sides, down most of the
  height, wider than a stroke), the other page is painted the paper's
  colour, and the report says which page was scored in `facing_pages`.
  Before, the lines of both pages were measured together and the report
  described neither. A one-page photo is read exactly as before.

- Fixed: neat handwriting that the OCR reads with 93% confidence or more is no longer turned into print and refused. A small trained line reader (`backend/print_hand.py`, 8-bit weights in `backend/models/print_hand.json`) looks at the ink, and the printed check's two confidence rules now apply only when it does not see handwriting. On the sample photo, five handwritten lines it used to drop are scored again; the typed letter, the tracing worksheet and the receipt's printed labels are still excluded. Trained on UJI Pen Characters (CC BY 4.0) and open-licence fonts; `scripts/print_hand_data.py` and `scripts/train_print_hand.py` rebuild it.

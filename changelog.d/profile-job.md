- Profiling job (#231): `.github/workflows/profile.yml` replays the benchmark pages (Layer A, no models) through `scripts/profile_benchmark.py`, once under cProfile and once under tracemalloc, every Monday, by hand and on pull requests labelled `perf`. The job summary shows the top 30 functions by cumulative time and the top 20 places holding memory at the peak, compared with the last run on main; a row that entered the top 10 or grew by more than 25% is marked. It never fails a build on timings, and keeps the raw `.prof` and both tables as an artifact. How to read it: docs/18-benchmark.md.

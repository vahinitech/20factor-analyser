- Size Consistency compares each word's small letters with the size
  expected where the word sits on the page, and leaves out what is not a
  small-letter size (#304): scraps under half the page's usual line height,
  lines whose small-letter band measured outside 0.15 to 0.8 of their own
  box (real writing gave 0.19 to 0.63 on the benchmark pages), and words
  with a digit. The expected size follows the page from top to bottom, so
  a phone held at an angle no longer reads as letters growing; that trend
  is fitted from the lines' places on pages of four lines or more, so one
  line of uneven words, or a second line written larger, still shows. The
  report's word-size marks use the same words. The sample page's Size rises
  from 5.1 to 6.4; on the 20 UJI pages it moves on four (one by 0.6, three
  by 0.1).

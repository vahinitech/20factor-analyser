- Each function that runs once per page now has its growth recorded (`backend/perf/complexity.json`, #230): what its n is (lines, pixels, graphemes, readings) and its declared big O, with a reason for anything above O(n log n). Passage alignment is O(n^2), edit distance between the line read and its passage line, and so are merging repeated readings, joining a line's pieces and finding the rows of a page from its ink (each row is masked over the whole page). A new CI job runs each function on synthetic inputs at n, 2n, 4n and 8n, takes the median CPU time of five runs, fits the slope of log time against log n, and fails when it is more than 0.3 above the declared class. The table is in the job summary and the `complexity` artifact.

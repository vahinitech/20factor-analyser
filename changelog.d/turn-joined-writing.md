- An upright list in joined-up writing (one or two words to a line, each
  word one piece of ink) is no longer refused as "on its side" or turned a
  quarter (#297 follow-up). The page-turn check took a whole joined word as
  its letter size, smeared the lines into columns, and read the page as
  running up and down. It now checks such a call again at the size of the
  words' height and leaves the page as it is when the two disagree. No
  benchmark or fixture page changes.

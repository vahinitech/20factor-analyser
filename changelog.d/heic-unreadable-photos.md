- A photo the browser cannot open no longer does nothing. On 2026-10-06 a visitor on Chrome for Mac, between JPEG uploads that worked (the first got a printed report), tried one iPhone HEIC photo five times: each time no preview, no message, "Run analysis" greyed out, and a rejected upload in the store. Chrome and Firefox now convert a HEIC photo to JPEG in the browser with libheif (libheif-js 1.23.5, LGPL-3.0), loaded from jsDelivr only for such a photo: about 520 KB compressed, the script pinned by SRI and the wasm file by SHA-384. If a photo still cannot be opened, a notice in the upload box says so; for HEIC it gives the ways to get a JPEG (Preview on a Mac, Most Compatible on an iPhone). The page reports that outcome (`heic` or `unreadable`) to vahinitech.com's weekly report. The background copy to the upload store sends only JPEG, PNG and WebP: a converted HEIC goes as its JPEG, another image the browser can open (HEIC in Safari) is converted to JPEG first (at most 4000 px on the long side, inside iOS Safari's canvas limit), and a file that cannot be opened, or a PDF, is not sent. `tests/unreadable-image.mjs` (`npm run test:unreadable`) covers it with a real HEIC fixture.

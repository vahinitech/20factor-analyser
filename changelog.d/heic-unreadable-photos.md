- A photo the browser cannot open no longer does nothing. On 2026-10-06 a visitor on Chrome for Mac, between JPEG uploads that worked (the first got a printed report), tried one iPhone HEIC photo five times: each time no preview, no message, "Run analysis" greyed out, and a rejected upload in the store. The upload box now says what happened; for HEIC it names the format and says to open the page in Safari or save the photo as a JPEG. The page reports the outcome (`heic` or `unreadable`) to vahinitech.com's weekly report. The background copy to the upload store sends only JPEG, PNG and WebP: another image the browser can open (HEIC in Safari) is converted to JPEG first (at most 4000 px on the long side, inside iOS Safari's canvas limit), and a file that cannot be opened, or a PDF, is not sent. `tests/unreadable-image.mjs` (`npm run test:unreadable`) covers it.

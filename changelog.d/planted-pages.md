- Added: `scripts/planted_pages.py` measures whether the report's marks land on a known mistake. It writes pages from real handwritten letters (UJI Pen Characters, CC BY 4.0, downloaded and checked, never committed), plants one mistake per page and reports how often a mark covers it, how tight the mark is and what the score did. The first results are in `docs/02-how-a-scan-works.md`, section 8.
- Added: `backend/letter_geometry.py` measures inside each line: a Theil-Sen baseline through the letters, the small-letter height along it, each word's distance from it, and small letters against that height. On planted pages it finds a word sunk below the line on 95% of pages (line boxes: 10%) and no longer counts a sloping line as larger writing. The report does not use it yet; candidate scores are printed beside today's.
- Changed: planted pages are drawn as photos by default (pen width that changes with pressure, uneven light, grain, blur, JPEG).

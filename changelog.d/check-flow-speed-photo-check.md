- The check flow is faster and warns about photos that would mislead the scores. The photo is uploaded as a JPEG capped at the server's 2600 px working size instead of a full-size PNG (1.2 MB to 236 KB and 6.1 MB to 757 KB on the test photos, same overall scores), the ~2 s of fixed progress animation is gone along with the three steps it animated, the wait is described honestly ("usually 5 to 20 seconds"), and a blurred or small photo gets a warning before upload. Darkness is not flagged: the server reads an evenly dark photo fine. The end-to-end recognition test now also checks the warning, the JPEG upload, the five-skill report with crops and the next step.

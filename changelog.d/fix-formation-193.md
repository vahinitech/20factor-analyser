- Letter Formation (1), Legibility (18) and Character Distinction (19) now notice a word that cannot be read (#193). The reader gives one confidence per line, so the least sure quarter of the words counts twice, each word's ink is checked for a scribble (strokes that cross and turn far more than the writer's other words), and with a copied passage each word not read as written counts 0. Legibility includes how surely the words were read, not only an average of other factors. Character Distinction no longer takes marks off for digits.
- Stroke Order (2) and Line Quality (4) are no longer scored from a photo, which cannot show them (#192). Like Dynamics (13 to 16) they report "Not measured" with method `sensor_required` and are left out of the Structure average; the pen flow is unchanged.

- Loops and letter zones are now marked not measured on a page with no English letters, such as a Telugu page, instead of scoring 0. Both measures count English letters, so the 0 was never a measurement; it lowered the Telugu overall score and put both in "skills to grow". Telugu overall scores rise as a result. English pages are unchanged.
- An upload no longer sends the English pangram as a reference passage by default. The server rewrites any read line close enough to the passage into the passage itself, which could replace a child's own words. A passage is sent only when one is picked.

- The free check asks "Who is checking?" before a page is read, with nothing pre-selected (DPDP Rules 2025, rule 10). A student needs a parent, guardian or teacher's tick; a grown-up's answer is remembered in the browser, a student's never. The answer goes with the photo as one word (`who`), and where `VAHINI_REQUIRE_WHO=1` the server refuses a free check without it (422 `who_required`) before reading the photo. A Pro key is not asked. Not stored or echoed.
- Names, phone numbers, emails, roll numbers and school names in the text read from a page are blanked (`[name]`, `[phone]`, ...) before a scan is cached or returned (`backend/reading/identifiers.py`). Scores do not change.

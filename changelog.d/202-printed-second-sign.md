- The printed check sets a line aside only when a second sign agrees:
  the line reader sees print, or the ink repeats like a font (the same
  letter overlaps to the pixel, the small letters keep one bottom line
  and height). Neat handwriting read confidently used to be removed by
  the rules alone: on 173 real phone photos (GNHK) 12.6% of handwritten
  words were set aside, now 4.4%; printed words kept rose from 23.8% to
  28.4%. Printed pages and short labels on forms still follow the rules
  (#202). The text classification says why in `print_check`.

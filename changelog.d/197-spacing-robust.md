- Word Spacing and Letter Spacing measure how far gaps stray from their
  median instead of the coefficient of variation, and score gaps that
  stand out on their own (#197). Word Spacing loses up to 2 points as
  gaps under half or over twice the median reach a tenth of the page.
  Letter Spacing compares each gap with its own word's, loses up to 1
  point for gaps over twice their word's, and up to 3 for words written
  with their letters apart. Neat writing and a planted spacing mistake
  now stand further apart, so the scales can later be set from neat
  pages without missing a planted mistake.

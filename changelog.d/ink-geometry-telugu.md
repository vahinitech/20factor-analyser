- Handwriting the reader cannot read can be measured from the ink (`backend/ink_geometry.py`), for the languages named in `VAHINI_INK_GEOMETRY_LANGS` (empty by default). On 20 pages of real Telugu handwriting PP-OCRv5 scored 7 of 80 lines; from the ink every line is found, the baseline is fitted through the letters' bottoms without the consonants written below the line, Size compares groups of five letters, and Word Spacing is measured when the passage copied gives each line's words (the planted wide gap found on 6 of 6 pages) and is "Not measured" otherwise. Factors that need the words read stay "Not measured". Printed pages are still refused. Measured by `scripts/ink_geometry_eval.py` and kept as private benchmark sets (docs/18-benchmark.md).
- Pieces of a line are joined against the piece nearest them, and joining repeats until nothing more joins, so a line in many pieces, or a sloping one, becomes one line. One English benchmark page moved (UPV_W48 read "the quick" for "the suick").

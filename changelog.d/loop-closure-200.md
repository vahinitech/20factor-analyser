- Loop Closure (factor 3) measures closed bowls in the ink and nothing else (#200). A hole counts as a bowl only inside the small-letter band, or for a bowl written tall when it starts on the baseline, so the loops of l, h, k and of the tails of g, j, y no longer stand in for an a or o left open. Each word counts at most as many closed bowls as it has letters with a bowl (a, b, d, e, g, o, p, q), so extra holes in one word cannot cover open bowls in another. A page with fewer than five such letters on measured lines is reported as not measured, where it used to be scored from the share of loop letters in the text. The planted open-loop pages are now caught for all 20 writers (was 17), and on the 20 real benchmark pages it now ranges from 4.3 to 10, with 3 pages at 10 instead of 16.

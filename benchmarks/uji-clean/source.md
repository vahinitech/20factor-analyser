# Source of the pages

Letters: UJI Pen Characters (Version 2), Prat, Castro, Llorens, Marzal and
Vilar, 2008, UCI Machine Learning Repository, doi:10.24432/C5FG8S,
licensed CC BY 4.0. Only the 20 writers of its published test split.

Each page is written in one writer's own letters by
`scripts/planted_pages.py` (photo style: pen width that changes with
pressure, uneven light, paper grain, blur and JPEG), kind `clean`, with
nothing planted: four of the six sentences in that script, in our cast's
words. They were made on 19 October 2026 and scored by vahinitech.com's
free check the same day.

The pages are committed, not made again on each run: a different JPEG or
OpenCV build would change the pixels a little, and the benchmark would
then report a change no code made. Children's photos are never added
here; the analyser does not store photos (owner decision, 2026-10-09).

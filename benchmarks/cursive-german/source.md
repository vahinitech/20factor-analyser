# Source of the pages

**What these pages are, and are not.** Joined Latin-letter handwriting
in German. One page is adult handwriting written in the GDR
Schulausgangsschrift (1968). The other three are older: lithographed
model copies of the Latin "englische Current" taught in Bavarian
schools in 1866, kept here only as precursors of the four German
school scripts, not as one of them. No page shows the Lateinische
Ausgangsschrift (1953), the Vereinfachte Ausgangsschrift (1969) or the
Grundschrift (2011): no openly licensed specimen of running text in
those three was found (see the end of this file).

Only running sentences were cropped: no drills, single letters,
alphabets, signatures or printed text. Each crop keeps only the
handwritten block, scaled down to 2600 pixels wide where wider. Nothing
else was changed.

## Precursor: August Berger, 21 Vorlegeblätter zum Schönschreiben in englischer Current (1866)

- August Berger, life dates not found. München: C. H. Beck, 1866.
  Lithographed copy sheets for older pupils ("für Schüler der
  Oberklassen"), German text in the Latin joined hand of the time.
- Scan: https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_DQBCAAAAcAAJ (Google Books
  scan, id DQBCAAAAcAAJ, copied to the Internet Archive in 2014).
- Rights: the Internet Archive item records "licenseurl:
  http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/". Published in 1866,
  so in the public domain in the USA. India: Berger's death year is not
  recorded, but he was an adult author in 1866, so he would have to have
  lived past about 120 for the work to be protected; in the public
  domain there too.
- For the owner: the scan was made by Google, whose front page on its
  own PDFs asks for non-commercial use. That is Google's request about
  its scan, not a copyright in the 1866 work, and the Internet Archive
  item carries the Public Domain Mark. The "Digitized by Google" mark
  is outside every crop. If the owner reads the brief's "not Google
  Books-only scans" as covering this, drop the three Berger pages.

## Not found, and what was searched

- **Lateinische Ausgangsschrift (1953)** and **Vereinfachte
  Ausgangsschrift (1969)**: Commons search and the categories by both
  names (no category exists under either name). The files there are
  alphabet charts and two- or three-letter comparisons
  (`Lateinische Ausgangsschrift - Übersicht.jpg`,
  `Vereinfachte Ausgangsschrift - Übersicht.jpg`, `- Bsp1.jpg`,
  `- Bsp2a.JPG` (the one word "lieben"), `- Bsp3a.JPG`, the `ß ...`
  files, SVG letter charts). None holds running text.
- **Grundschrift (2011)**: no Commons category; search returns only a
  pronunciation file (`De-Grundschrift.ogg`). Nothing on the Internet
  Archive.
- **Schulausgangsschrift, published model**: `Schriftproben der
  Schulausgangsschrift und ihren Varianten.png` (Renate Tost, 1987) has
  five lines of running model text, but it was uploaded by another
  account (Thirunavukkarasye-Raveendran, 2018) under `{{self|cc-by-sa-4.0}}`
  with Tost (born 1937) named as author and no permission ticket on the
  page. The licence chain is unverified, so it is not used. Tost's other
  files (`Schulausgangsschrift 1968.png`, `Alphabet der
  Schulschrift-Kursiv.jpg`) are alphabets. `Ausgangsschrift der DDR
  1958.png` is an alphabet.
- **Older Latin models**: Sütterlin's Latin Ausgangsschrift
  (`Lateinische Ausgangsschrift von Sütterlin.jpg`, `"Neuer Leitfaden
  für den Schreibunterricht" - Abbildung 10`) and Kuhlmann 1926 (`Fritz
  Kuhlmann, Erarbeitung der Lateinschrift.jpg`) are alphabets or word
  drills. Kurrent and Sütterlin German-script sources were skipped as
  the brief says.
- Internet Archive searches: titles with Schreibunterricht,
  Schreibvorlagen, Schönschreiben, Ausgangsschrift, Schreibschrift,
  Grundschrift, Sütterlin, "lateinische Schrift". August Berger's
  other books (`bub_gb_aUhCAAAAcAAJ`, 1854; `bub_gb_DgBCAAAAcAAJ`, 1866)
  are further precursor candidates and were not looked at page by page.

The written lines of each crop are in `set.json` (`page_text`), lower

Handwriting marking sheet

One sheet per page. Mark before you see any analyser score, and without talking to the other therapist about this page. Write the page number only: never the child's name.

Page number: Your code (T1, T2 ...):

Marks: a whole number from 0 to 10 for each skill, the scale the report uses: 0 to 4 Learning, 5 to 6 Improving, 7 to 8 Mastering, 9 to 10 Strong. Leave a box empty if you cannot judge that skill from what you have.

#Skill: what to look atMark 0-10
Letter shapes and size
1Letter Formation Accuracy: Letters are formed the way they are taught and are easy to recognise on their own.The analyser measures: shape regularity proxy.
2Stroke Order Consistency: Each letter is built the same way every time it appears. Best judged while watching the child write.The analyser measures: stroke order proxy.
3Loop Closure: Letters with a bowl or loop (a, d, g, o, p, q, e) are closed, with no gap at the top.The analyser measures: loop-bearing character consistency.
4Line Quality (Smoothness): Strokes are smooth and even, without wobble or tremor.The analyser measures: stroke smoothness proxy.
5Size Consistency: Small letters (a, c, e, n, o) keep the same height across the page.The analyser measures: letter-height consistency.
6Ascender / Descender Control: Tall letters (b, d, h, k, l) reach up and tails (g, p, y) reach down by the right amount.The analyser measures: zone balance.
Spacing and lines
7Baseline Alignment: Words sit on the line instead of floating above it or dropping below it.The analyser measures: baseline drift.
8Word Spacing: Spaces between words are clear and about the same each time.The analyser measures: inter-word spacing regularity.
9Letter Spacing: Letters inside a word are evenly spaced, neither crowded nor spread apart.The analyser measures: intra-word spacing proxy.
10Margin Discipline: Lines start at the same place on the left.The analyser measures: left margin consistency.
11Line Straightness: Each line of writing runs level across the page.The analyser measures: line slope stability.
12Vertical Alignment: Upright strokes lean the same way from one line to the next.The analyser measures: stroke tilt stability.
Movement (only if you watched the child write)
13Speed Consistency: The child writes at a steady pace. Only if you watched.The analyser measures: speed proxy from stroke regularity.
14Pressure Consistency: The pen presses evenly, not hard then light. Only if you watched or can feel the paper.The analyser measures: pressure proxy from ink variance.
15Stroke Continuity: Words are written in one flow, without breaks the child did not mean. Only if you watched.The analyser measures: continuity proxy from word morphology.
16Pen Lift Frequency: The pen leaves the paper only where it should. Only if you watched.The analyser measures: pen-lift proxy from segmentation.
Slant, legibility and neatness
17Slant Consistency: Letters lean the same way across the page.The analyser measures: slant variation.
18Legibility Score: Someone who does not know the child can read the page.The analyser measures: composite readability.
19Character Distinction: Letters that look alike (a and o, n and h, u and v, 1 and l) can be told apart.The analyser measures: character separability proxy.
20Overall Neatness: The page as a whole looks tidy and organised.The analyser measures: layout neatness composite.

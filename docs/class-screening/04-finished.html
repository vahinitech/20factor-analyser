What a flag means. A flag says that, on this one page, two or more of the five skills are at the first stage, Learning, and names them. It is a reason to look at the page and at the child's other work, not a finding about the child and not a diagnosis. One rushed or tired page can be flagged; a child who finds writing hard can write one good page. We have not yet measured how often these flags agree with a therapist's own view.

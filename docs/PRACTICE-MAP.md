# Practice map: exercises and tips for every factor

Generated by `scripts/practice_map.py` from `backend/practice_catalog.py`
(served as catalogue version `a2126ecdadf2fe1f65b7`). Do not edit by hand:
change the catalogue, then run the script. Design and rules:
[PRACTICE-FRAMEWORK.md](PRACTICE-FRAMEWORK.md).

A report gives practice only for the skills it flags. Per skill, Free shows
1 exercise and 1 tip, Pro up to 2 and 2, taken in level order and
moving on at each re-check. How many tries a day follows the stage:

| Stage | Score | Tries |
|---|---|---|
| Learning | below 4.5 | 15 a day for 7 days, then check again |
| Improving | 4.5 to 6.9 | 10 a day for 7 days, then check again |
| Mastering | 7 to 8.4 | 5 a day for 7 days, then check again |
| Strong | 8.5 and up | 10 once a week, then check again |

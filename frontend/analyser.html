Not sure what to write or photograph? Read this first
1What to write

Write 4–6 full lines of normal joined or print handwriting on plain or ruled paper. A short paragraph beats a single word: the more real letters, words and lines, the more the 20 factors have to measure.

No idea what to write? Copy any of these:

  • “The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Pack my box with five dozen liquor jugs.” (every letter of the alphabet)
  • “The sun is bright today. We play in the green garden. Birds sing in the tall trees.” (easy primary practice)
  • A few lines from a book, a diary entry, or a letter to a friend, anything in your natural hand.
2Taking the photo
  • Fill the frame with the writing: get close, leave little background.
  • Flat & straight-on. Hold the phone parallel to the page, not at an angle (we auto-correct small tilt, but flat is best).
  • Even light, no shadow from your hand or phone across the page.
  • Sharp focus: tap the screen on the text before you shoot.
  • Plain or single-ruled paper; avoid busy grids or coloured backgrounds.
How Vahini reads your page, so the score makes sense:
  • Handwriting only. Printed text (a letterhead, a form's printed labels, a textbook line) is detected and left out of scoring; only your handwriting is measured.
  • English today. Telugu and other scripts are not scored yet. Choose Telugu above and the check tells you, instead of scoring a guess.
  • When there is enough clear handwriting: size evenness, spacing, baseline, slant, loop closure, line straightness, neatness.
  • Read line-by-line when the words are recognised: spelling, punctuation, mixed cursive/print, stray capitals, repeated-letter consistency.
  • Needs the Battu pen: speed, pressure, pen-lifts and flow. These are not scored from a photo.

The free check

What you get from one photo

  • Five skills with evidence from your page. See letter shapes, size, baseline, word spacing and readability.
  • Up to three priorities. The parts of your handwriting that would improve the page most, in the order to practise them.
  • Coaching and short drills. What to change for each priority, with a few minutes of practice you can print and do at a table.
  • Spelling suggestions. Common English spelling mistakes, when the words on the page are clear enough to read.
Four steps

How the handwriting check works

  1. Write four to six lines on plain or ruled paper with a dark pen, in your everyday handwriting.
  2. Take the photo from straight above in even light, with the whole page in the picture. A tilted photo makes straight lines look slanted.
  3. Upload it and press Analyse my handwriting. A photo (JPG, PNG and other image types) or a PDF, up to 10 MB.
  4. Practise your first priority for a week, then check again with the same pen and paper and compare the two reports.
Questions

Before you upload

Is the handwriting check free?

Yes. The free check covers five writing skills with evidence and practice priorities. The full review covers more factors; pen-only factors need sensor data.

What happens to my photo?

Your photo is sent to the server for analysis. Saving pages is optional: choose Keep my pages and confirm through the emailed link to save them for progress tracking. Saved pages are deleted 12 months after your last check. Details are in the privacy statement.

Which handwriting can it read?

English today. Telugu is not scored yet: choose Telugu and the check says so instead of guessing. The practice worksheets already cover Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada and Malayalam.

How accurate is it?

The scores come from the picture, so a clear, straight, evenly lit photo matters more than anything else. A photo cannot measure pen pressure or speed; those factors are not scored from a photo. We have not established equivalence to human teacher evaluation. Each factor's page explains what is measured and the target.

Does it say anything about personality?

No. It measures how readable and even the writing is: letter shapes, spacing, alignment, size and slant. It is not graphology, and it is not a medical or educational assessment.

What would the Vahini pen add?

A photo shows the finished page. The Vahini pen, in development, records how the page was written while you write on ordinary paper, to capture movement and pressure that a photo cannot measure.

Questions people ask

About the free AI handwriting check

Plain answers for parents and teachers. Free, no sign-up.

What is an AI handwriting check?

You upload one photo of a handwritten page. Vahini's server finds the lines, words and letters, measures them, and gives a score out of 100 with the first skills to practise. The free check scores five skills: letter shapes, letter size, writing on the line, spacing between words and readability.

What does the score out of 100 mean?

85 and above is Strong, 70 to 84 is Mastering, 45 to 69 is Improving and below 45 is Learning. Each skill also gets a score out of 10 on the same stages. Read what your handwriting score means.

Can I check my child's handwriting?

Yes. Use a page your child wrote in the usual way, four to six lines, and check a new page after a week of practice. See the handwriting check for kids.

What are the most common handwriting problems?

Letters formed differently each time, small letters of mixed heights, words that run together and writing that drifts off the line. The free report looks for these on your own page and says which one to practise first.

Does it check cursive handwriting?

Yes, joined writing is read too. See the cursive handwriting check.

How do I take a good photo of handwriting?

Lay the page flat in daylight and hold the phone straight above it, with the whole page in the picture. A tilted photo bends the lines and changes the scores.

How long does the check take?

Usually well under a minute once the photo is uploaded.

How do I improve my handwriting after the check?

Practise the first skill for a few minutes every day for a week, then check a new page. Bheema's free practice plan makes the worksheets.

Can it detect dysgraphia?

No. It describes the page, not the child, and it does not diagnose anything. If writing stays tiring or painful, a teacher or an occupational therapist can help. Read the signs a child needs handwriting help.

Do I need to sign up or pay?

No. The free check needs no account and no payment. You can check up to three pages a day.

Can I talk to a person about my child's writing?

Yes. Ask our team and we reply with advice for your child's page.

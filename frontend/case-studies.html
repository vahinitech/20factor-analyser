Three writers, before and after practice

Three common handwriting problems. For each one you see the score before practice and the score after a few weeks of the practice drills from the report. The same page always gets the same score, so when a score goes up, the writing really improved.

No real children appear here. Each page was made by computer to show one writing habit. Every number below comes from the real scoring engine, the same one that scores your page. The results are saved in docs/examples/ and you can make them again yourself: python docs/examples/generate_examples.py.

Score bands: Strong 8.5 and above · Good 7.0 to 8.4 · Developing 4.5 to 6.9 · Needs support below 4.5. These are the same bands printed on every report. Every drill named above comes from the report itself: the practice plan is built from the same measurements as the scores. This material is for learning and practice. It is not a medical test.
